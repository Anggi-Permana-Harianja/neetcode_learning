Approach
s = "ADOBECODEBANC", t = "ABC" → "BANC"
  1. Count required characters from t; track how many distinct required counts are still 'missing'.
  2. Expand the window's right edge, decrementing need[ch] and reducing missing when a needed char is fully covered.
  3. Once missing reaches 0, the window contains all of t — try to shrink from the left while it stays valid, recording the best window.
  4. Continue expanding right until s is exhausted.
