Approach
intervals = [[1,2],[2,3],[3,4],[1,3]] → 1 (remove [1,3])
  1. Sort intervals by END time — a classic greedy interval-scheduling trick.
  2. Greedily keep the interval that ends earliest, since it leaves the most room for future intervals.
  3. For each subsequent interval, if it starts before the last kept interval ends, it overlaps — remove it (increment the counter) and keep the earlier end.
  4. Otherwise keep it and update the 'last end' boundary.
