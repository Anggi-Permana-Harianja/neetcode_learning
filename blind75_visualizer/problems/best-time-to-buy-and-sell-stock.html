Approach
prices = [7,1,5,3,6,4] → 5 (buy at 1, sell at 6)
  1. Track the lowest price seen so far while scanning left to right.
  2. At each price, compute the profit if sold today (price - min_price).
  3. Keep the best profit seen; update the running minimum as you go.
