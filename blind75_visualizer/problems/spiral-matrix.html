Approach
[[1,2,3],[4,5,6],[7,8,9]] → [1,2,3,6,9,8,7,4,5]
  1. Track four shrinking boundaries: top, bottom, left, right.
  2. Walk the top row left→right, then the right column top→bottom, then (if rows remain) the bottom row right→left, then (if columns remain) the left column bottom→top.
  3. After each side, shrink the corresponding boundary inward.
  4. Repeat until the boundaries cross.
