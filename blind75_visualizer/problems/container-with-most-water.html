Approach
height = [1,8,6,2,5,4,8,3,7] → 49
  1. Start pointers at both ends — the widest possible container.
  2. The area is limited by the shorter of the two lines, so track the best area seen.
  3. Move the pointer at the shorter line inward — moving the taller one can only shrink width without helping height.
  4. Repeat until the pointers meet.
