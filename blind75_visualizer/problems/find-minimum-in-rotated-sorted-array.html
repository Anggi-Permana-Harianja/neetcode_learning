Approach
nums = [4,5,6,7,0,1,2] → 0
  1. Binary search, comparing nums[mid] to nums[right] to decide which half is sorted.
  2. If nums[mid] > nums[right], the minimum is in the right half (past mid) — move left = mid + 1.
  3. Otherwise the minimum is at mid or to its left — move right = mid.
  4. When left == right, that's the minimum.
