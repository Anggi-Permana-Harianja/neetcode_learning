Approach
1→2→3→4 becomes 1→4→2→3
  1. Find the middle of the list with slow/fast pointers.
  2. Reverse the second half of the list in place.
  3. Merge the first half and reversed second half by alternating nodes.
  4. Stop once the second (shorter or equal) half is exhausted.
