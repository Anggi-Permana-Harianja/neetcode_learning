Time O(n) Space O(h)
Approach
root = [4,2,7,1,3,6,9] → [4,7,2,9,6,3,1] (mirrored)
  1. Recursively invert the left and right subtrees.
  2. Swap the (already-inverted) left and right children at each node.
  3. Base case: an empty subtree inverts to itself (None).
