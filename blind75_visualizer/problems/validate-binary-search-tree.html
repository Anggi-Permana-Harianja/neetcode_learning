Time O(n) Space O(h)
Approach
[5,1,4,null,null,3,6] → False (4 < 5 but is in right subtree)
  1. It's not enough to compare a node only to its direct children — every node in a left subtree must be less than ALL of its ancestors, not just its parent.
  2. Carry a valid (low, high) range down through the recursion.
  3. At each node, check low < node.val < high; recurse left with high = node.val, recurse right with low = node.val.
  4. An empty subtree is always valid.
