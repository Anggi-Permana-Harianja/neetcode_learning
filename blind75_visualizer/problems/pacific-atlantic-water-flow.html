LeetCode #417 · Find cells from which water can flow to both the Pacific and Atlantic oceans.

Approach
heights grid, oceans border top/left (Pacific) and bottom/right (Atlantic)
  1. Instead of checking 'can this cell reach the ocean' (expensive per cell), reverse it: flow uphill from each ocean's border cells inward.
  2. DFS from every Pacific-adjacent border cell, moving to neighbors with height >= current (since water flows downhill, reversed means uphill-or-equal is reachable).
  3. Do the same from Atlantic-adjacent border cells into a separate visited set.
  4. A cell reachable from both oceans is part of the answer.
