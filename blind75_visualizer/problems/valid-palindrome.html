Approach
s = "A man, a plan, a canal: Panama" → True
  1. Use two pointers starting at both ends of the string, moving inward.
  2. Skip any character that isn't alphanumeric on either side.
  3. Compare the two characters case-insensitively; mismatch means not a palindrome.
  4. If the pointers meet without a mismatch, it's a palindrome.
