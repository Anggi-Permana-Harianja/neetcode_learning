Approach
intervals = [[1,3],[6,9]], newInterval = [2,5] → [[1,5],[6,9]]
  1. Since intervals are already sorted, copy over every interval that ends before the new one starts, unchanged.
  2. Then merge every interval that overlaps the new one, expanding newInterval's bounds to cover them all.
  3. Append the fully-merged interval once no more overlaps remain.
  4. Copy over the rest of the untouched intervals that start after the new one ends.
