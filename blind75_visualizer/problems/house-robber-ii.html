House Robber II

Medium
DP

LeetCode #213 · Same as House Robber, but houses are arranged in a circle.

solution.pyO(n) time · O(1) space

Approach
nums = [2,3,2] → 3
  1. Because the first and last houses are now adjacent, they can't both be robbed.
  2. Reduce to two separate House Robber runs: one excluding the first house, one excluding the last.
  3. The answer is the max of those two runs (this correctly handles the wrap-around).
  4. Special-case a single house, since slicing would otherwise produce empty ranges.
